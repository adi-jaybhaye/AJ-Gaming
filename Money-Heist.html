How to play — Money Heist: Silent Grid

Your objective: collect all the loot 💰 and reach the vault 🏦 without being caught by guards or stepping into active lasers.

Controls

  • Arrow keys — move (desktop)
  • Mobile D-pad — tap or hold to move
  • Pause — freeze guards & lasers from HUD
  • Mute — toggle SFX from HUD

Gameplay Rules

  • Walls (W) block movement — you cannot pass through.
  • Loot (💰) disappears when collected. Collect all to unlock the vault.
  • Lasers (🔴) toggle on/off — stepping on an active laser = instant reset.
  • Guards patrol according to their paths. If they catch you, you reset to the start.
  • Reach the vault (🏦) after collecting all loot to clear the level.

Tips

  • Watch patrol timing — move between gaps when guards turn away.
  • Use Pause to study laser timings or plan moves on mobile.
  • Collect loot near the vault last if guards patrol nearby.

Accessible: this panel supports keyboard focus, Escape closes it, and screen-readers are announced automatically.
