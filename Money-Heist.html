How to Play — Money Heist: Silent Grid

Collect all the loot 💰 and reach the vault 🏦 without being caught by guards or stepping into active lasers.

Controls

  • Arrow keys — move (desktop)
  • D-pad — tap or hold to move (mobile)
  • P — Pause / Resume
  • R — Restart level
  • I — Toggle instructions

Rules

  • Walls block movement — you cannot pass through.
  • Collect all loot 💰 to unlock the vault 🏦.
  • Lasers 🔴 toggle on/off — stepping on one while active = reset.
  • Guards patrol set paths. If they catch you, you start over.

Tips

  • Watch guard patrol timing and move in the gaps.
  • Use Pause to study laser timing.
  • Collect loot near the vault last if guards are nearby.
